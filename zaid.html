Zaid Season Crops (March - June)

Zaid is a short duration summer season between Rabi and Kharif. These crops are grown in the hot summer months with irrigation, taking advantage of the gap between two main seasons.

🍉 Watermelon

Growing Period: 70-100 days

Ideal Temperature: 25-35°C

Water Requirement: 400-600 mm

Uses: Fruit consumption, hydrating vegetable

Tips: Requires well-drained, fertile soil. Drip irrigation works best. Harvest when ripe for sweet taste.

🍈 Muskmelon

Growing Period: 70-90 days

Ideal Temperature: 25-35°C

Water Requirement: 300-400 mm

Uses: Fruit consumption, high nutritional value

Tips: Plant on ridges for better drainage. Regular watering ensures sweet fruits. Shade helps in extreme heat.

🥒 Cucumber

Growing Period: 60-90 days

Ideal Temperature: 25-32°C

Water Requirement: 350-500 mm

Uses: Vegetable consumption, salads, pickles

Tips: Requires trellising or support structures. Regular harvesting promotes more fruit production.

🍅 Tomato

Growing Period: 80-100 days

Ideal Temperature: 20-30°C

Water Requirement: 400-500 mm

Uses: Vegetable consumption, sauces, pastes

Tips: Requires staking for support. Mulching conserves moisture. Water at soil level to prevent diseases.

🌶️ Chili / Pepper

Growing Period: 90-120 days

Ideal Temperature: 25-35°C

Water Requirement: 350-500 mm

Uses: Spice production, vegetable consumption

Tips: Requires well-drained soil. Mulching helps retain moisture. Harvest regularly for continuous production.

🥔 Potato (Summer)

Growing Period: 90-120 days

Ideal Temperature: 15-25°C

Water Requirement: 400-600 mm

Uses: Staple vegetable, food grain

Tips: Plant seed potatoes in February-March. Use certified disease-free seeds. Provide adequate irrigation in summer.
