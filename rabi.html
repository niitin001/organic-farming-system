Rabi Season Crops (November - April)

Rabi crops are sown after the monsoon season and harvested in spring. These crops grow in cooler months and are primarily grown in northern India.

🌾 Wheat

Growing Period: 120-140 days

Ideal Temperature: 15-25°C

Water Requirement: 400-600 mm

Uses: Staple food grain, flour production, bread

Tips: Sow in November for best results. Ensure proper soil preparation and timely irrigation.

🌾 Barley

Growing Period: 110-130 days

Ideal Temperature: 15-25°C

Water Requirement: 300-500 mm

Uses: Food grain, animal feed, malt production

Tips: Drought tolerant. Requires less water than wheat. Good for marginal lands.

🤎 Mustard

Growing Period: 90-120 days

Ideal Temperature: 15-25°C

Water Requirement: 300-450 mm

Uses: Oil production, condiment, animal feed

Tips: Drought resistant. Apply adequate phosphorus for better pod setting.

🤎 Gram (Chickpea)

Growing Period: 100-120 days

Ideal Temperature: 15-25°C

Water Requirement: 350-500 mm

Uses: Protein source, pulses, flour (besan)

Tips: Fixes nitrogen in soil naturally. Requires minimal fertilizer inputs.

🌾 Linseed

Growing Period: 100-120 days

Ideal Temperature: 15-23°C

Water Requirement: 300-450 mm

Uses: Oil production, linen fiber, animal feed

Tips: Prefers well-drained soil. Excellent for rotational cropping.

🌾 Peas

Growing Period: 80-100 days

Ideal Temperature: 10-20°C

Water Requirement: 300-400 mm

Uses: Vegetable, pulse, protein source

Tips: Plant in October-November. Support structures may be needed for climbing varieties.
