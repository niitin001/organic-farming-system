Soil Types and Suitable Crops

India has diverse soil types that support various crops. Understanding soil helps farmers select the right crop for better yield and sustainability.

Black Soil

Rich in clay and moisture-retaining capacity. Suitable Crops: Cotton, Soybean, and Sunflower.

Alluvial Soil

Found in river plains, rich in nutrients. Suitable Crops: Rice, Wheat, Sugarcane, and Pulses.

Red Soil

Low in nitrogen but rich in iron. Suitable Crops: Groundnut, Millets, Potato, and Cotton.
