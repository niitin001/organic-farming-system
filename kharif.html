Kharif Season Crops (June - October)

Kharif crops are sown with the monsoon rains and harvested after the rainy season ends. These crops thrive in the warm, humid climate of monsoon months.

🌾 Rice

Growing Period: 120-150 days

Ideal Temperature: 20-30°C

Water Requirement: 1000-1500 mm

Uses: Staple food grain, widely consumed across India

Tips: Requires flooded fields. Use quality seeds and proper irrigation management.

🌽 Maize (Corn)

Growing Period: 90-120 days

Ideal Temperature: 21-27°C

Water Requirement: 500-750 mm

Uses: Animal feed, food grain, starch production

Tips: Ensure proper spacing and timely weeding. Use hybrid seeds for better yield.

🤍 Cotton

Growing Period: 160-180 days

Ideal Temperature: 21-30°C

Water Requirement: 600-900 mm

Uses: Textile industry, fibers

Tips: Requires well-drained soil. Monitor for pests and diseases regularly.

🌾 Soybean

Growing Period: 75-110 days

Ideal Temperature: 20-30°C

Water Requirement: 450-600 mm

Uses: Protein source, oil production, animal feed

Tips: Rotate crops to maintain soil fertility. Apply appropriate fertilizers.

🌾 Bajra (Pearl Millet)

Growing Period: 60-90 days

Ideal Temperature: 25-35°C

Water Requirement: 400-600 mm

Uses: Food grain, animal feed, nutritious cereal

Tips: Drought resistant. Requires minimal inputs and suits marginal lands.

🌾 Groundnut

Growing Period: 90-120 days

Ideal Temperature: 24-28°C

Water Requirement: 400-600 mm

Uses: Oil production, protein source, food

Tips: Requires well-drained soil. Proper pod development requires good calcium and boron.
