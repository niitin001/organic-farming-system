Season Wise Crops

Crops in India are mainly classified into three seasons — Kharif, Rabi, and Zaid. Click on any season to see detailed crop information.

Kharif Season (June - October)

Sown with monsoon and harvested after rainy season. Examples: Rice, Maize, Cotton, Soybean, Bajra.

Rabi Season (November - April)

Grown after monsoon and harvested in spring. Examples: Wheat, Barley, Mustard, Gram.

Zaid Season (March - June)

Short-duration summer crops. Examples: Watermelon, Muskmelon, Cucumber.
